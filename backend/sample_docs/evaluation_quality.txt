Retrieval Evaluation and Iterative Quality Improvement
======================================================

High-quality knowledge assistants need measurement, not vibes. A retrieval and evaluation
pipeline scores both document retrieval and final answer relevance.

Useful metrics
--------------
- Retrieval hit-rate: fraction of expected keywords present in top-k passages.
- Lexical overlap with a reference answer.
- Semantic relevance between question and generated answer via embedding similarity.
- Composite score blending the above signals.

Improvement loop
----------------
Start with a weak baseline such as returning a single raw chunk. Then enable agentic planning,
broader top-k retrieval, and tool-assisted synthesis. Compare composite scores. Teams commonly
observe roughly a 25% relative lift in answer relevance after iterating on retrieval depth,
chunking, and grounded synthesis prompts.

Persist evaluation reports so regressions are visible when documents or models change.
